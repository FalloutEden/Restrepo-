We want every order to feel like a good purchase. If something arrived wrong, broken, or just isn’t right, we’ll work with you to make it right. This policy explains how returns and refunds work at Black Vault Apparel.

Return window

You have 30 days from the delivery date to request a return.

How to start a return

  1. Email support@blackvaultapparel.com with your order number and a brief description of the issue.
  2. If the issue is a defect, wrong item, or shipping damage, please attach photos.
  3. We’ll reply within 1–2 business days with next steps and (if needed) the return address.

Return shipping costs

  • Defective, wrong, or damaged items: we cover return shipping.
  • All other returns (size, change of mind, fit): the customer is responsible for return shipping.

Print-on-demand items

Our apparel is printed and assembled to order. Because each item is made specifically for you, we are not able to restock it. This affects how returns work:

  • Manufacturing defects, print errors, or damage in transit: we’ll send a free replacement or issue a full refund. Email a photo of the issue and we’ll handle it.
  • Wrong size ordered: our size charts are listed on every product page. If you ordered the wrong size, you can return the item within 30 days for a refund minus original shipping. Return shipping is paid by the customer.
  • Change of mind: we accept returns within 30 days as long as the item is unworn, unwashed, and has all tags attached. The customer pays return shipping.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, we’ll send a confirmation email. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–10 business days. Depending on your bank, it may take additional time for the credit to appear on your statement.

Late or missing refunds

If you haven’t received an expected refund, first check with your bank or credit card company — processing times can vary. If you’ve done that and still don’t see the refund, email support@blackvaultapparel.com and we’ll look into it.

Exchanges

The fastest way to get a different size or item is to place a new order and return the original. This avoids the wait for inspection before re-shipping.

Sale and final-sale items

Items marked “final sale” on the product page are not returnable except in the case of defects, errors, or shipping damage.

Lost or stolen packages

Once a package is marked delivered by the carrier, responsibility passes to the customer. If a package is missing or shows incorrect tracking, contact support@blackvaultapparel.com and we will help you open a claim with the carrier.

Questions

Email support@blackvaultapparel.com. We respond within 1–2 business days.
