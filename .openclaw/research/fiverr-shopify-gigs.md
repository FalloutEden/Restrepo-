## 2. Winning title formula
Pattern across every winner: **"I will [verb] [primary keyword] [niche qualifier] with [tool keywords]"** under 80 chars. Live examples: *"I will create shopify print on demand pod store with printify"*, *"I will setup print on demand shopify store"*. Stack tool keywords (Shopify + Printful + Printify) to capture multi-tag search. Description first sentence must repeat primary keyword (Jobbers 2026 guide).

## 3. Gallery images that convert
Community data: refreshed thumbnail + video = **+30% impressions, 2x orders**. Winning 2026 formula: high-contrast bg, human face pointing at <5 words of text, 1280x769. Slot order: (1) before/after store screenshot, (2) founder face + outcome claim, (3) deliverable mockup grid. Video thumbnail overrides image — record a 30s store walkthrough.

## 4. 3-tier package template (synthesized from top performers)
- **Basic $149 / 3d / 1 rev** — Shopify store, theme install, 10 POD products, logo placement, basic pages.
- **Standard $349 / 5d / 2 rev** — everything in Basic + 25 products + Printful integration + on-page SEO + email capture + 5 apps. *This is the middle anchor — 60-70% of buyers land here.*
- **Premium $649 / 7d / 3 rev** — 40 products + custom logo + premium theme + speed optimization + 8 apps + 30-day support. (Premium exists to make Standard look like a deal — pricing-mistake #1 per Fiverr Community is huge unjustified jumps; keep each step ~2x.)

## 5. Gig extras buyers actually buy
Industry benchmarks (Fiverr community + Jobbers): 24h express **15-25% attach**, source files **20-30%**, priority support **10-15%**, extended commercial license **5-10%**. Gigs with extras run **30-50% higher AOV**. Recommended adds:
- 24h rush delivery — **+$75** (target 20%)
- Extra 10 products — **+$50** (target 40%)
- Logo design — **+$60** (target 35%)
- Speed/SEO pass — **+$80** (target 25%)
- 30-day post-launch support (SaaS funnel hook) — **+$99** (target 50%, mirrors our subscription price for anchoring)

## 6. Response + delivery cadence
Top Rated requires **<1h response, 90%+ rate**. Success Score weights: 35% buyer-sat, 25% value, 20% comms, 15% timeliness, 5% repeat. Delivery: basic 3d beats 5d on CTR; never list >7d on basic. Auto-responder on first message is mandatory.

## 7. FAQ block (close objections)
Five-question template winners use: (1) Do I need a Shopify account first? (2) Do I provide the products or do you? (3) What if I want changes after delivery? (4) Will the store be ready to take orders day-one? (5) Do you offer ongoing support? — Q5 is the SaaS-funnel handoff: answer "yes, $99/mo manages products, mockups, and order routing" and link the upsell.

## SaaS funnel hook
Bake the $99/mo into Premium's "30-day support" and Extra-5's "ongoing management" — buyers who attach support convert ~3x to subscription per generic SaaS funnel benchmarks. Title every deliverable folder with our brand so the merchant sees the dashboard on day one.
