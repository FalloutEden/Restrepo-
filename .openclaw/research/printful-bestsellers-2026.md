## 2. Niches: low competition + high LTV
- **Pet niche** ($157B/yr market): 45-65% margins, top sellers = pet-parent tee (ID 71), mug (ID 19), wall portrait poster (ID 1). Breed-specific designs convert at 3-5x generic.
- **Fitness/run-club**: tri-blend tee (ID 162), AOP leggings, beanie (ID 266). LTV is high — repeat buyers stock up.
- **Hobby-specific** (anime, trades, hunting, gaming, faith, nurses, teachers): UMAI ($130K/mo) used tees + hoodies + wall art only. Tight niche beats broad.
- **Memorial/personalized**: posters + mugs, 60%+ margins, high gift-AOV.

## 3. Print method routing
- **DTG**: 3001 (ID 71), 64000 (ID 12), 18500 (ID 146), 18000 (ID 145), mugs, posters, stickers, phone cases.
- **Embroidery (premium look, no fade)**: snapback (ID 99), dad hat (ID 323), beanie (ID 266), Gildan crewneck (ID 145) for left-chest logos.
- **All-Over Print (AOP, cut-and-sew)**: leggings, joggers, AOP hoodie, bandanas, drawstring bags. Use ONLY when design is the product (linear graphics, repeating patterns). AOP has 5-7 day longer fulfillment; don't lead with it.

## 4. Margin gotchas
- **Mugs**: $6.49 US shipping for 1, then $3.50/add — kills single-item AOV. Bundle 2+ or price >=$22.
- **Framed posters (ID 1 framed variants)**: $10.49 shipping single. Avoid for cart-of-one.
- **AOP items**: fulfilled only at specific facilities, expect 5-7 business days production + 5-20 shipping. Don't promise <2 weeks.
- **EU routing**: orders to EU customers ship from Latvia/Spain; orders to US ship from NC/CA/MX. Cross-region orders add 10+ days — geo-restrict storefront if scaling globally.
- **Phone cases**: device-model SKU sprawl; pick 3-4 newest iPhones only on launch.
- **Premium garments (Bella+Canvas, Champion)**: only worth it if brand positioning supports $30+ retail. Gildan for value brands.

## 5. Recommended first-catalog mix for new merchant (8-15 SKUs)
Core 8: 3001 tee (ID 71) x2 designs, 18500 hoodie (ID 146), 18000 crewneck (ID 145), snapback (ID 99) OR beanie (ID 266), white mug (ID 19), matte poster (ID 1), kiss-cut sticker (ID 358).
Stretch 7 (if niche supports): tri-blend tee (ID 162), tough phone case (ID 601), black mug (ID 300), die-cut sticker (ID 957), dad hat (ID 323), AOP leggings, tote bag.
Skip on day 1: framed posters, AirPods cases, swimwear, dresses, blankets — low search demand for new brands.
