# Etsy as a Sales Channel: Ads + Conversion Brief (2026)

Use this when onboarding a brand that wants Etsy alongside Shopify+Printful.

## 1. Etsy Ads — Budget + ROAS (new shop)
- CPC for POD niches: $0.20–$0.50. Minimum daily budget $1.
- Start at $1–$3/day. Only promote listings that already have 1+ organic sale — ads amplify proven SKUs, they do not rescue dead ones.
- Realistic month 1 ROAS: 0.5x–1.0x (you are buying algorithm data, not profit). Month 2–3 target 1.5x–2.5x once dud listings are pruned.
- Margin rule: do not run ads unless gross margin ≥ 30% pre-ad. 15% ad-cost budget on a 30% margin product leaves 15% net. Bake Offsite Ads' 15% fee into pricing — it's mandatory once you cross $10K trailing-12-mo.

## 2. Photos that convert
- Hero (image 1): clean white/neutral BG with the design dominant. Highest CTR in search grid.
- Slots 2–7: lifestyle on-model, in-scale (mug in hand, poster on a wall with furniture), flat-lay, detail close-up, size chart, packaging. Listings with stylized mains see up to 314% higher CTR vs flat-white-only in handmade/personalized categories.
- Required: 2000px+ wide, 4:3 ratio, first image safe-zone for the mobile crop.

## 3. SEO — titles + tags
- Titles: natural language, first 40 chars must contain the primary buyer-intent phrase (mobile cutoff). Stop keyword-stuffing — Etsy 2026 algorithm penalizes it.
- Pattern: `[Specific Descriptor] [Product Type] — [Use Case/Recipient], [Material/Style], [Occasion]`
  Example: `Minimalist Line-Art Cat Mug — Gift for Book Lovers, Ceramic Coffee Cup, Birthday Present`
- Tags: 13 slots, all multi-word long-tail (`gift for cat mom`, not `cat`). Mine from eRank/Marmalead. Mix 3 broad + 7 mid + 3 ultra-niche.

## 4. Pricing sweet spots
- Apparel/mugs: $22–$28 retail wins volume. Below $20 the Etsy + Offsite Ads + Printful stack eats margin.
- Premium tier ($40–$120): canvas, framed prints, large posters, personalized memorabilia. Math works better here — fees are a smaller % of AOV. A shop at 60% AOV>$40 typically out-nets a shop at 90% AOV<$30.
- Personalization adds a 20–40% price tolerance (buyers expect to pay more).

## 5. Star Seller (rolling 90 days)
- 95% message response <24h, 95% on-time ship w/ tracking, 4.8+ rating, 5+ orders OR $300 sales (lowered from 10 orders in July 2025).
- Earliest possible badge: day 91 after first sale. Auto-checked the 1st of each month.

## 6. Niches under-saturated in 2026
- Personalized coasters, keychains (couples/anniversary), pet-memorial wall art, adaptive/sensory-friendly apparel, hobby-specific hoodies (pickleball, disc golf, sourdough), B2B digital templates, framed wedding/family prints. Avoid generic "funny mug," "live-laugh-love," cat/dog generics — fully saturated.

## 7. Killers to avoid
- Trademark traps: NEVER use `fits Stanley`, `Disney`, `Bluey`, sports teams, song lyrics — 3 IP strikes = permanent ban. Brand legal teams scan daily.
- AI-art disclosure: must select "Designed by" not "I made it" if AI-generated. Hiding it = Creativity Standards violation.
- Shipping: missing tracking on >5% of orders tanks Star Seller and surfaces in algorithm.
- Don't list 100+ SKUs day 1 — Etsy flags as reseller. Open with 15–25, add weekly.
