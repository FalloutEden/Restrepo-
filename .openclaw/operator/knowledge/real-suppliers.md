# Real premium apparel suppliers — verified contacts and reality

Researched 2026-05-04 against actual sources. **POD (Printful, Apliiq) is a v1 / soft-launch tier — not the premium endgame.** Real Travis Mathew / Live Lucky / Lacoste / James Perse tier requires direct factory relationships. This document is the path.

The honest tradeoff: factory direct gives you 40–60% lower COGS and genuine premium feel. It costs you upfront capital ($2–15k per first run depending on tier), longer lead times (6–16 weeks), and quality control overhead (samples, factory comms).

## Tier 1 — Top-tier factories used by Lacoste / Ralph Lauren / Travis Mathew

These are the actual factories from the Google search. They produce for the brands you're benchmarking. **They will work with you, but on their terms.**

### Southern Textile Network (STN Perú) — Lima, Peru

The Peruvian Pima cotton specialist. Known clients on their public site: **Lacoste, Ralph Lauren, Abercrombie & Fitch.** Operating since 1998.

- **Specialty:** Polos, T-shirts, hoodies, shirts, light jackets — men's, women's, kids
- **Certifications:** WRAP, GOTS, GRS, U.S. Cotton Trust Protocol, Cotton Leads
- **Sustainability:** 2030 sustainability plan aligned with UN SDGs; verified carbon and water footprints
- **Realistic MOQ:** Not published. Best guess from industry knowledge: 200–500 per style for new clients; sometimes negotiable to 100 for premium positioning. **You have to email and ask.**
- **Lead time:** 90–120 days from approved sample to delivery
- **Capital range:** $5–15k for first 100-unit run of one style
- **Contact:** contact@perustn.com · +51 1 617-2525 · Av. Defensores del Morro 2066, Chorrillos 15066, Lima
- **Website:** [perustn.com](https://www.perustn.com/)

How to approach: send a short brand intro (you're a US premium essentials brand, currently launching, looking to move 1–2 hero SKUs from POD to Pima cotton private label), attach a tech pack (or rough specs — they'll guide you), ask for sample MOQ and pricing on a heavyweight tee + a piqué polo. Respond within 24 hours when they reply — Peruvian factories appreciate professionalism.

### Confecciones Textimax — Santa Anita, Lima, Peru

Lower-profile than STN but listed on Pietra's vetted supplier network. Founded 1991, exports to USA / Germany / Italy / Hong Kong, 7 production facilities in Lima.

- **Specialty:** Tees, shirts, crews, knits, hoodies (cotton-rich)
- **MOQ:** Pietra listing says "1+" (likely aggregator-driven; direct probably 100–300/style)
- **Lead time:** 120+ days
- **Contact:** textimax@textimax.com.pe · (+51 1) 713-7000 · Av. Huarochirí 151, Santa Anita, Lima 43
- **Pietra listing:** [pietrastudio.com/sourcing/suppliers/confecciones-textimax-sa](https://www.pietrastudio.com/sourcing/suppliers/confecciones-textimax-sa)

Use case: backup to STN if STN's MOQ is too high or lead time doesn't fit. Smaller scale = more receptive to small brands.

### TAV Limited (TAL Apparel Group) — Vietnam

The Hong Kong-headquartered TAL Group's Vietnam factory. Used by Travis Mathew, Lululemon (some SKUs), Under Armour for performance polyester apparel.

- **Specialty:** Knit and woven shirts; performance polyester for golf/athleisure
- **Scale:** 5,000+ employees, exports 16M units/year, LEED Gold certified
- **MOQ:** Realistically **500–2,000 per style.** This is an enterprise factory; small brands will not get easy access.
- **Recommendation:** **Skip for v1–v2.** This is where you go after $100k+ revenue and a proven SKU. Not the right fit while you're building.
- **TAL Group website:** [talapparel.com](https://www.talapparel.com/)

## Tier 2 — Sweet-spot for a small premium brand RIGHT NOW

These are explicitly designed for the gap between POD and full factory. **This is where Black Vault should land for hero SKUs after POD validation.**

### ASBX — Portugal (Porto region)

- **MOQ:** 50–100 per style/color (fully custom cut-and-sew). Some blank styles **no MOQ** — useful for sampling/test runs.
- **Quality tier:** Premium. They explicitly position themselves as the alternative to POD for brands going up-tier.
- **Lead time:** 6–10 weeks
- **Capital:** $1,500–4,000 for a 50–100 unit run depending on style
- **Website:** [asbx.pt](https://asbx.pt/)
- **Why it fits BV:** Portuguese-made (genuine premium signal), MOQ low enough for capital-constrained launches, embroidery-friendly.

### Athleisure Basics — Portugal

- **MOQ:** 70 pieces per style/color (white-label customization)
- **Lead time:** 3–5 weeks (faster than ASBX)
- **Quality tier:** Premium blanks, similar to ASBX positioning
- **Website:** [athleisurebasics.com](https://athleisurebasics.com/)
- **Use case:** Faster iteration than ASBX; good for a first private-label test run

### Create Fashion Brand (Portugal)

- **MOQ:** 50 pieces per model/color
- **Quality tier:** Premium ready-to-brand blanks
- **Website:** [createfashionbrand.com](https://createfashionbrand.com/)

### ExploreTex — Portugal

- **Quality tier:** Premium full-package production
- **MOQ:** Higher than ASBX (likely 100–200), but full-package design support
- **Website:** [exploretex.com](https://exploretex.com/)

## Realistic phased path for Black Vault

| Phase | When | What | Capital | Suppliers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0 — Now** | $0 revenue, today | Publish 23 POD drafts. Validate demand. | $0 | Printful (existing) |
| **1 — Validation** | Months 1–3 | Identify the 2 top-selling SKUs from POD. Modest ad spend ($150–300/wk). | $1–3k ad spend | Printful |
| **2 — Hero upgrade** | Months 3–4 (after $5–10k revenue) | Move top 2 SKUs to Portugal small-batch (ASBX or Athleisure Basics). 50–100 unit run each. | $3–6k inventory | ASBX, Athleisure Basics |
| **3 — Line expansion** | Months 6–9 (after $20–40k revenue) | Move remaining hero SKUs to Portugal + open conversation with Textimax for cotton-heavy pieces. | $10–20k inventory | ASBX + Textimax |
| **4 — Premium tier** | Year 2+ (after $100k+ revenue) | Direct relationship with STN Perú. Multi-SKU runs, Lacoste-tier feel. | $50k+ inventory | STN Perú |

**Skip the trap:** Going from POD to STN directly without ASBX validation is high-risk capital. STN has 90–120 day lead times — if your SKU bombs, you're sitting on $15k of unsold inventory in their warehouse for months. Portugal small-batch lets you fail fast with $2k.

## What needs the user (operator can't do this)

1. **Email STN, Textimax, ASBX** — operator can draft the email; user has to send + handle replies
2. **Tech pack creation** — basic spec sheet (fabric weight in oz/yd², construction details, measurements). For BV's heavyweight tee: "6.5 oz combed ring-spun cotton, 220 GSM, side-seamed, 1×1 rib collar, single-needle edge stitch, embroidered chest left placement"
3. **Sample order** — first interaction with any factory is a sample order. Costs $50–300 per sample. User has to feel and approve before committing capital.
4. **3PL setup** — once you move to private label, you need a fulfillment partner (ShipBob, ShipMonk, or local Utah 3PL). Operator can research; user has to sign contract.
5. **Initial capital** — phase 2 requires $3–6k, phase 3 requires $10–20k.
