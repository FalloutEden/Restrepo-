## What I built so this stops happening

### 1. Brand-fit filter — `lib/brand-fit-filter.ts`

Regex gate that runs in the pipeline **between opportunity_router output and materializeProduct**. Rejects:

- **Occupation-specific** (40+ patterns: nurse, veteran, therapist, architect, creative director, dog/german shepherd dad, faith/scripture, etc.)
- **Service products** (consulting, automation setup, prep kit, dashboard, database, course, etc.)
- **Digital downloads** (PDF, ebook, planner, printable, sticker pack, etc.)
- **Inspirational quotes** (hustle, grind, queen, boss babe, rockstar, etc.)
- **Holiday-themed** (Christmas, Valentine's, Mother's Day, etc.)
- **For LockLayer specifically:** anything without security/IoT vocabulary
- **For Black Vault specifically:** all of the above plus apparel-only enforcement

Tested against 12 known-bad titles (all 5 just-deleted + 7 historical slop): **all rejected with reasons.** Tested against 8 known-good SKUs: **all accepted.** 20/20 pass at `scripts/test-brand-fit-filter.ts`.
